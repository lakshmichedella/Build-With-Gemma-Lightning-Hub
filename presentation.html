The Clinical Friction in ER Handovers

Handing over critical patients under high stress leads to lost details, delayed triage, and miscommunicated history.

❌ Traditional ER Handover Friction

  • Chaotic verbal dictation during ambulance transport.
  • Nurses manually re-type symptoms while assessing patients.
  • Past medical history & severe allergies get overlooked.
  • Doctors inherit incomplete notes without prioritized acuity rankings.

⚡ Gemma Lightning Hub Solution

  • Voice + Multimodal Vision: Dictation & injury photo auto-structured in seconds.
  • FHIR-Lite Persistence: Single SQLite record updated across all personas.
  • Visible ESI Reasoning: 3-step Chain-of-Verification prevents over/under-triage.
  • Ranked Doctor Queue: Live acuity ranking with ICD-10 billing coding.

Gemma Reasoning Engine & Safety

Ensuring clinical decision support is transparent, verifiable, and safety-audited.

🧠 Chain-of-Verification (CoV) Triage

Step 1: Red Flag ExtractionRed Flags
Identifies compromised vitals, hemodynamic instability, or trauma alerts.
Step 2: Preliminary ESI ScoreScore 1-5
Proposes initial ESI level based on expected ED resource utilization.
Step 3: Self-Critique GuardrailsAudit
Critiques preliminary score against over-triage and under-triage clinical traps.
Final Output: Verified ESI BadgeFinal Badge
Provides final ESI score with a concise 1-sentence rationale saved to SQLite.
